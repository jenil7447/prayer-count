Privacy Policy for Prayer Count

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Prayer Count is a prayer and chanting counter application designed to help users count prayers or chants, save sessions, view daily records, and manage personal app settings.

1. Information Stored by the App

Prayer Count stores certain information locally on your device so that the app can provide its features. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • Prayer or chant session names entered by you
  • Prayer or chant counts
  • Session dates and daily prayer records
  • Your selected target count
  • Audio and vibration preferences
  • Your selected app theme preference

2. How Your Information Is Used

The information stored by Prayer Count is used only to provide the app's functionality, such as maintaining your counts, saved sessions, daily history, and preferences.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

Prayer Count is designed to store the information listed above locally on your device. Based on the current version of the app, this information is not transmitted to a developer-operated server or cloud database, and the app does not require you to create an account or sign in.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Permissions

The app may use the Android vibration capability when you enable vibration feedback while counting. This permission is used only to provide vibration feedback within the app.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version of Prayer Count does not use advertising, analytics, authentication, or a developer-operated backend service for collecting your app data.

6. Data Deletion

Because Prayer Count stores its app data locally and does not maintain a user account on a developer-operated server, there is no server-side account or profile to delete. You can manage or remove the app's locally stored data using Android's device/app settings or by uninstalling the app, subject to Android's handling of backups and device data.

7. Children's Privacy

Prayer Count does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated server or account system. The app does not require users to provide personal information to use its core counting features.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time if the app's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
